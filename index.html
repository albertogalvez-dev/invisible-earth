    A quick guide

    See the change.

    Choose a place

    Pick a location to follow a story built from NISAR radar observations. Use the arrows to move through its chapters, or choose Film to watch automatically.

    Explore for yourself

    Drag to orbit and scroll to zoom. Right-drag to move across the map; on touchscreens, use two fingers. Choose Back to the shot to return to the guided view.

    Read the data

    Radar compares passes made on different dates, not continuous footage. Displacement is along the satellite's line of sight. Relief and motion may be exaggerated for visibility; surface changes are not always ground movement.

    Check the evidence

    Open About for the data, dates, interpretation and sources. The signal label identifies real observations or a synthetic fallback.
